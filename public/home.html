What does Bachs do?

Bachs lets you get paid by customers anywhere. They pay by bank transfer, mobile money or stablecoins, and the money lands in your account in your currency.

Which currencies can I get paid in?

Naira, cedis, shillings and US dollars. You can hold a balance in each and withdraw when you want.

Do I need a developer to use Bachs?

No. Payment links, invoices and the WordPress plugin work without code. The API is there when your team wants to build it into your product.

How do I get started?

Sign up with your email or Google, verify your business, then send your first payment link or invoice.
